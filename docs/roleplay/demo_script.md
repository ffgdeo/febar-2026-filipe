# PromoFlow roleplay demo script

## Opening — 60 seconds

Cleany is a fictional CPG manufacturer. Its planners need to choose profitable promotions, but history, forecasts, events, economics, and inventory plans are fragmented. I will show how PromoFlow connects those inputs into one governed decision journey. The outcome is better trade-spend allocation, faster planning, and earlier visibility of availability risk.

## Demo — 8 to 10 minutes

### Overview

**Tell:** I will start with the decisions requiring attention.

**Show:** pending approvals, upcoming events without plans, approved promotions, and promotions at inventory risk.

**Tell:** leaders see the portfolio queue; planners know where to act first.

### Past Promotion Analysis

**Tell:** Before recommending a plan, we need evidence of what worked.

**Show:** filter by product, market, mechanic, and success; highlight margin, ROI, uplift, trade spend, and lost sales.

**Tell:** Cleany can distinguish volume from profitable growth and see when availability diluted a successful commercial idea.

### Upcoming Promotions

**Tell:** Next I will show plans already imported from the TPM system.

**Show:** event filter, plan status, readiness, receipts, and projected shortfall.

**Tell:** this is where a commercially attractive plan is stopped for review before commitment, without PromoFlow pretending to automate supply execution.

### Opportunity Inbox and Scenario Studio

**Tell:** Now I will create a new scenario from a model-scored opportunity.

**Show:** select product/market/event; choose a recommendation; compare versions. Point out that list price and unit cost are governed, while planner-controlled inputs are mechanic, discount, and timing.

**Tell:** each alternative uses the same governed facts and transparent economics, making the comparison reproducible.

### Decision Workspace and Unity Gateway

**Tell:** I will approve the preferred version and create an executive-ready brief.

**Show:** availability check, decision record, AI-generated narrative, model/prompt metadata.

**Tell:** the model explains the decision but cannot change the economics or approval. The human remains accountable.

### Genie

**Tell:** Finally, stakeholders can interrogate the same governed products in natural language.

**Show:** ask for upcoming approvals with inventory risk; expand generated SQL if available.

**Tell:** business users gain self-service without creating a parallel semantic definition.

## Close — 60 seconds

PromoFlow connects Lakeflow, Unity Catalog, MLflow, Lakebase, Unity Gateway, Genie, and Databricks Apps in one workflow. For a pilot, I recommend one category, two clusters, and one planning cycle, measuring planner time, positive-margin spend, unresolved shortfall at approval, forecast error, and adoption.
