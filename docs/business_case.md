# Business case

Cleany planners decide which SKU, market, event, mechanic, timing, and discount deserves trade investment. Today those decisions are slowed by fragmented performance history, forecasts, event calendars, product economics, and inventory plans.

PromoFlow creates one journey: learn from completed promotions; find event-aligned opportunities; compare scenarios using governed price and cost; check inventory, receipts, commitments, and safety stock; record approval; and generate a governed AI brief.

## Buyer KPIs

| Outcome | KPI | Prototype evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Clearer decision queue | Pending approvals | 15 imported plans pending approval |
| Better trade allocation | Incremental margin / trade spend | $71.5M / $30.0M synthetic historical portfolio |
| Opportunity capture | Modeled future incremental margin | $642K total; $267K across READY opportunities |
| Faster planning | Time from opportunity to comparable scenario | One workflow instead of cross-system analysis |
| More profitable plans | Expected incremental margin and ROI | Visible for each opportunity and version |
| Fewer availability surprises | Constrained plans and shortfall units | 12 plans and 5,205 units in current imported-plan set |
| Protected revenue | Historical revenue at risk | $3.0M across 217 promotions with recorded lost sales |
| Trusted decisions | Model/input/narrative/audit reproducibility | Persisted in UC and Lakebase |

Historical values are realized synthetic results; opportunity values are modeled future potential and are not presented as savings. A customer pilot would compare realized incremental margin, trade ROI, forecast error, revenue-at-risk reduction, planner hours, and adoption against prior cycles and a matched control where feasible.
